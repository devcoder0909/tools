## Overview
Preserve the existing light, emerald-accented utility site. New search guides extend its editorial layout; this is not a redesign.

## Colors
Use the existing emerald links, slate body text, white surface and subtle borders.

## Typography
Keep the existing font stack. Guides use readable body text, one H1, descriptive H2 sections and underlined inline links.

## Layout
Use the shared header/footer and max-width article container. Tables scroll within their container on mobile. No new animation or client-side framework.

## Elevation & Depth
Use borders rather than new decorative effects.

## Shapes
Match existing rounded controls and article containers.

## Components
Reuse Layout and Breadcrumbs. Guide contents and related-tool links are ordinary HTML anchors.

## Do's and Don'ts
Keep important answers in the initial HTML. Do not turn guides into keyword lists, duplicate tool landing pages, or obscure tool access with overlays.
