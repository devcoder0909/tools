## Does Converting JPG to PDF Reduce Image Quality?

Creating a PDF from JPG images does not automatically mean that the photos must be visibly degraded. The resulting appearance depends on how the conversion engine places and encodes the source images in the PDF. ZixTool's conversion workflow is designed to place the selected images into the PDF document without deliberately resizing them to a lower resolution.

However, the PDF's final appearance can still depend on the original image dimensions, the source JPEG's existing compression, page-fit settings, and the PDF generation process. A low-resolution or blurry source image cannot become sharper simply by converting it to PDF.

For the clearest result, use the original, highest-quality image available and inspect the downloaded PDF before using it.

## JPG vs. PDF: Which Format Should You Use?

JPG and PDF serve different purposes:

- **Choose JPG** when you need an individual image for photo sharing, image editing, or a website that specifically requests a JPEG file.
- **Choose PDF** when you want to combine several images into one document, preserve a page-based layout, or share a file that is convenient to print and review.

Converting JPG to PDF does not turn the image text into selectable text. The image remains an image placed on a PDF page. If you need searchable or selectable text from a scan, you need an OCR (optical character recognition) process in addition to PDF conversion.
