## Tips for Preserving Clear Signature Legibility Under 20KB

When you compress image to 20KB, fine ink lines can sometimes appear faint or pixelated if compressed incorrectly. Follow these best practices:

- **Sign on Clean White Paper with Dark Ink:** Blue or black ballpoint or gel pens provide maximum contrast against white paper. Avoid textured or lined notebook paper.
- **Crop Tightly Around the Signature:** Remove excess blank margins before compression. Cropping tightly dedicates 100% of the 20,480-byte allocation to actual ink lines rather than empty white paper.
- **Keep Native Aspect Ratio:** Avoid stretching or distorting width and height proportions so portal image validation scripts do not reject your upload for aspect ratio distortion.
- **Adjust Contrast Before Compression:** Ensure the background is pure white (#FFFFFF). Any gray shadows from smartphone camera lighting consume valuable bytes in the JPEG compression matrix.
