## Why Portals Enforce a Strict 20KB Limit

Application submission systems receive millions of applicant files during recruitment windows. Strict byte limits ensure portal databases remain responsive:

- **Candidate Signatures (10KB to 20KB):** Portals often require scanned signatures between 10KB and 20KB with dimensions around 140x60 pixels. Compressing past 20KB prevents "File size exceeded" error messages during form submission.
- **Biometric Thumb Impressions:** Left and right thumb impression scans must remain sharp enough to verify ridge patterns while adhering to the 20KB ceiling.
- **Bank Exams & Civil Service Forms:** National and state recruitment boards (such as SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and state public service commissions) immediately reject uploads larger than 20KB.

## Tips for Preserving Clear Signature Legibility Under 20KB

When shrinking signatures to fit under 20KB, fine ink lines can sometimes appear faint. Follow these best practices:
- **Sign on Plain White Paper with Dark Ink:** Blue or black ballpoint and gel pens provide maximum contrast against white paper.
- **Crop Tightly Around the Signature:** Remove excess empty margins before compression to dedicate maximum byte budget to the signature ink strokes.
- **Keep Native Aspect Ratio:** Avoid stretching or distorting width and height proportions so validation systems do not flag aspect ratio mismatches.
