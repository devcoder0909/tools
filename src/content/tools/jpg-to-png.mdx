A common question users ask is: *Why is my converted PNG file 2x to 3x larger than the original JPG?*

- **Lossy vs. Lossless Algorithms:** JPEG compresses photographic data aggressively by discarding high-frequency color variations that human eyes rarely notice. PNG stores full 24-bit uncompressed RGB color data with lossless compression.
- **Preparing for Clean Edits:** The increase in byte size is the natural trade-off for arresting compression decay. PNG acts as a lossless container, making it ideal for editing masters in Photoshop, GIMP, Figma, or Canva.
