## What do I need on a final worth 20 percent?

If you have an 88% going into the final and want a 90% overall, you need 98% on a final worth 20%. The completed work contributes 88 × 0.80 = 70.4 percentage points. You need another 19.6 points from the exam, so divide 19.6 by 0.20 to get 98.

With the same current grade and exam weight, a target of 85% requires 73% on the exam. To aim for a letter grade, use the numerical cutoff printed in your syllabus.

## Which grade from my gradebook should I enter?

Use your average for everything except the final, with those completed assignments weighted as your instructor specifies. If your course portal counts an untaken exam as a zero, use the average that excludes that placeholder. This calculation assumes the final is the only assessment left.

## How do points-based courses work?

Suppose you have earned 420 of 500 points and the exam is worth 100 more. A target of 85% requires 510 of the 600 possible course points, so you need 90 points on the final. When the result falls between two whole points and the instructor awards whole points only, round up.

If your syllabus assigns separate weights to categories such as labs and exams, use the category weights when computing your current grade. Two assignments worth 100 points each can contribute different amounts to the course total.
