### Can someone know if I viewed or downloaded their WhatsApp DP?
No. Viewing a contact's profile picture or downloading it does not send read receipts or notifications to the owner. WhatsApp does not notify users when their profile photo is viewed or saved.

Furthermore, if you take a thumbnail screenshot from the chat conversation list instead of opening the profile picture full screen, the resolution will be limited to thumbnail dimensions (`320 × 320` pixels).
