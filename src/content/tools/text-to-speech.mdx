## How to Use Text to Speech in 3 Steps

Transform written documents and paragraphs into natural spoken audio:

1. **Paste or Enter Text:** Type or paste any article excerpt, email draft, or study notes into the text box above.
2. **Select Voice & Adjust Rate:** Pick your preferred speech synthesis voice from the dropdown, and adjust the playback speed and vocal pitch sliders.
3. **Listen Instantly:** Click **Play Audio Voice** to hear your text read aloud immediately using your device's native speech synthesis hardware.

### Key Use Cases for In-Browser Text to Speech

- **Proofreading Written Work:** Catching awkward sentence structures, repetitive words, and grammatical typos by listening to your text read aloud.
- **Accessibility & Vision Assistance:** Listening to long articles, web documentation, or terms of service without eyestrain.
- **Language Pronunciation Practice:** Hearing foreign vocabulary and phrases spoken in native system voice accents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which voices are available?
The available voices depend on your operating system (Apple Siri voices on Mac/iOS, Microsoft David/Zira on Windows, Google voices on Android).

### Does this tool upload my text to an AI server?
No. Audio synthesis runs entirely inside your browser using the native HTML5 Web Speech API (`SpeechSynthesis`).
