## The Temperature Conversion Formula

To convert degrees Celsius to degrees Fahrenheit, multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 (or 1.8) and then add 32:

```
°F = (°C * 1.8) + 32
```

To convert backwards from Fahrenheit to Celsius, subtract 32 and divide by 1.8:

```
°C = (°F - 32) / 1.8
```
