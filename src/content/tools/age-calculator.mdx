## The Mathematics of Chronological Age: Leap Years & Calendar Math

Calculating chronological age with exact precision is more complex than simple arithmetic because calendar months have non-uniform lengths ($28, 29, 30,$ and $31$ days) and leap years occur cyclically:

### 1. The Gregorian Leap Year Algorithm
Under the international Gregorian calendar, a year is a leap year (containing 366 days, with February 29) if:
- The year is evenly divisible by $4$, **and**
- The year is **not** divisible by $100$, **unless**
- The year is also evenly divisible by $400$.

For example, the year $2000$ was a leap year (divisible by $400$), whereas $1900$ was not a leap year. The years $2024$ and $2028$ are leap years, while $2025, 2026,$ and $2027$ are standard 365-day years.

### 2. Precise Borrowing Logic for Days and Months
To compute chronological age between a Birth Date $(D_b, M_b, Y_b)$ and a Target Date $(D_t, M_t, Y_t)$:
1. **Day Calculation:** If $D_t < D_b$, we borrow days from the month immediately preceding $M_t$. The number of borrowed days is not an arbitrary $30$, but the exact number of calendar days in that preceding month (e.g., $31$ days if borrowing from July into August, or $28/29$ days if borrowing from February into March).
2. **Month Calculation:** If $M_t < M_b$ (after adjusting for any borrowed day), we borrow $12$ months from $Y_t$.
3. **Year Calculation:** $Y = Y_t - Y_b$ (after adjusting for any borrowed month).

A leap day adds one day to the elapsed day count whenever the interval crosses 29 February.

## Global Age Reckoning Traditions: Western vs Traditional Asian Systems

While the international Gregorian standard counts age starting at zero on the day of birth and increments by one on each subsequent birthday, historical cultural traditions vary:

- **International Chronological Age:** Newborns are zero years old at birth. An individual turns 1 year old exactly 365 (or 366) days later on their first birthday.
- **Traditional East Asian Age Reckoning:** Historically in China, Korea, and Vietnam, a baby was considered 1 year old on the day of birth, and everyone gained an additional year simultaneously on New Year's Day (Lunar New Year or Solar New Year). Consequently, a child born in late December would legally be considered 2 years old within days of birth.
- **Modern Harmonization:** In June 2023, South Korea officially adopted the international chronological age standard for all administrative, civil, and judicial affairs, replacing traditional age reckoning across government documentation.
