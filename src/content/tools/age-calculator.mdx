## How Exact Age is Calculated

Calculating chronological age accurately requires accounting for uneven calendar month lengths (28, 29, 30, and 31 days) as well as leap years:

1. **Subtracting Days:** If the target day is smaller than the birth day, days from the preceding month are borrowed based on the exact number of days in that specific calendar month.
2. **Subtracting Months:** If the target month is earlier in the year than the birth month, 12 months are borrowed from the target year.
3. **Subtracting Years:** Target calendar year minus birth year minus any borrowed year adjustment.
