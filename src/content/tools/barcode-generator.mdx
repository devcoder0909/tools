### Standard Barcode Symbologies Explained

- **Code 128:** High-density alphanumeric barcode standard widely used in global logistics, shipping containers, and healthcare packaging.
- **EAN-13 & UPC-A:** Point-of-sale retail standards scanned at commercial cash registers worldwide for consumer goods.
- **Code 39:** General-purpose industrial barcode frequently employed by government agencies, automotive manufacturing, and military supply chains.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can retail laser scanners read these barcodes?
Yes. As long as you print the barcode at adequate physical size with high contrast on non-reflective paper, standard optical and laser barcode scanners can read it.

### Do I need to buy official UPC numbers to sell retail products?
If you plan to sell products in major international retail stores (like Walmart or Amazon), you must obtain certified GS1 company prefixes and barcodes.
