## GST Calculation Formulas

### 1. Adding GST (Exclusive Mode)
When adding GST to a net product price, calculate total GST and add it to the base:

```
GST Amount = (Net Initial Price * GST Rate) / 100
Gross Total Amount = Net Initial Price + GST Amount
```

### 2. Removing GST (Inclusive Mode)
When extracting embedded tax from an all-inclusive invoice total:

```
Net Base Price = Gross Total Amount / (1 + (GST Rate / 100))
GST Amount = Gross Total Amount - Net Base Price
```
