## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is Words Per Minute (WPM) calculated?
WPM is traditionally standardized as: $\text{WPM} = \frac{\text{Total Characters Typed} / 5}{\text{Time Elapsed in Minutes}}$.

### What is touch typing?
Touch typing is typing without looking at the keyboard keys, relying on muscle memory and the tactile home row bumps (`F` and `J`).
