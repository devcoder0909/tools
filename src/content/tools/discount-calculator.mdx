## How Retail Discounts Are Calculated

Calculating sale prices and savings involves standard markdown formulas:

1. **Calculate Savings:**
   $$\text{Savings} = \text{Original Price} \times \left(\frac{\text{Discount \%}}{100}\right)$$
2. **Calculate Discounted Price:**
   $$\text{Discounted Price} = \text{Original Price} - \text{Savings}$$
3. **Add Sales Tax (Optional):**
   $$\text{Final Total} = \text{Discounted Price} + \left(\text{Discounted Price} \times \frac{\text{Tax \%}}{100}\right)$$

*Worked Example:* A jacket priced at \$120 is on sale for 25% off with an 8% sales tax:
- Savings: $\$120 \times 0.25 = \$30.00$
- Price Before Tax: $\$120 - \$30 = \$90.00$
- Sales Tax: $\$90 \times 0.08 = \$7.20$
- Final Out-of-Pocket Price: $\$90.00 + \$7.20 = \$97.20$
