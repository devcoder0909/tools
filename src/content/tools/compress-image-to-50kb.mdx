## When to Compress an Image to 50KB

A file size cap of 50KB (51,200 bytes) is one of the most common requirements for official identity verification across government, academic, and professional submission systems:

- **Passport & Visa Portals:** International visa appointment systems, consular e-visas, and passport application platforms enforce a 50KB ceiling on applicant facial photographs.
- **State Recruitment & Civil Service Portals:** Public recruitment bodies, police recruitment boards, and state employment exchanges mandate applicant headshots between 20KB and 50KB.
- **University Examination Boards:** College entrance examinations and board exam registration portals reject profile pictures that exceed 50KB to preserve bandwidth during nationwide registration spikes.

## Technical Guidelines for 50KB Passport Photos

To ensure your compressed 50KB photo passes automated portal validation checks:
- **Neutral White or Off-White Background:** Most portals require a solid light background with no shadows or patterns.
- **Centered Head and Shoulders:** Your face should occupy between 70% and 80% of the overall frame height.
- **Preserve Facial Features:** Our engine preserves sharp eye clarity, skin tone graduation, and hairline edges while trimming unnecessary metadata and high-frequency noise.
- **Standard Dimensions:** Recommended pixel dimensions for passport photos are typically 200 x 230 pixels or 600 x 600 pixels depending on the specific agency.
