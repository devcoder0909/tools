### Why Modern Websites Convert JPG to WebP

- **Google Core Web Vitals Optimization:** Serving WebP images directly addresses Google's Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) performance recommendations, helping web pages rank higher in Google search results.
- **Mobile Network Performance:** WebP files transmit up to 35% faster over 4G and 5G cellular connections, significantly reducing bounce rates for e-commerce shops and content publishers.
- **Storage Conservation:** Hosting web galleries, user avatars, or photo portfolios in WebP format cuts AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, or hosting disk consumption by approximately one-third.
