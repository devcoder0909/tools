## Technical Specifications: JPG vs WebP Architecture Comparison

The architectural differences between 1992 JPEG baseline encoding and Google's VP8-derived WebP format explain why WebP achieves superior compression efficiency:

| Feature / Metric | Universal JPG / JPEG Standard | Google WebP Next-Gen Format | Technical Advantage of WebP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Compression Algorithm** | 8 × 8 Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) | VP8 intra-frame macroblock prediction | Eliminates blocky boundary grid artifacts |
| **File Footprint Savings** | Baseline standard (100% baseline) | 25% to 35% smaller at equal SSIM | Massive reduction in transferred network bytes |
| **Edge & Gradient Ringing** | High-contrast edges develop mosquito noise | Smooth gradients with advanced deblocking | Clean text, logos, and high-frequency edges |
| **Alpha Transparency** | Not supported (transparent pixels turn white) | Full 8-bit alpha channel transparency | Replace heavy PNG graphics with lightweight WebP |
| **Color Quantization** | YCbCr 4:2:0 / 4:4:4 chroma subsampling | Advanced linear chroma prediction filter | Preserves rich reds, purples, and fine line art |
| **Browser Compatibility** | 100% universal across all legacy systems | 98.4% global browser market share | Safe for production deployment on all modern sites |
