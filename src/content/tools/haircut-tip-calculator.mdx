## How much should you tip for a haircut? Standard salon guidelines

In the United States and Canada, personal care and grooming services carry established tipping conventions:

- **15% Tip (Standard Baseline Service):** Acceptable for a basic, quick clipper cut or student salon haircut where minimal styling is involved.
- **20% Tip (Good / Attentive Haircut):** The golden industry standard for professional barbers and salon hairstylists who consult on style, trim precisely, and blow-dry.
- **25% Tip (Exceptional Service, Complex Color, or Holiday Bonus):** Recommended for intricate fade work, beard shaping, balayage, complex highlights, or when visiting your regular stylist during the December holiday season.
- **$3 to $5 Cash Tip for Shampoo Assistants:** If a junior assistant washes and conditions your hair prior to the cut, it is customary to hand them a separate $3 to $5 cash tip directly at the sink station.

## Frequently asked questions about barbershop and salon tipping

### Do you tip if the stylist is the salon owner?
Historically, old etiquette manuals stated that salon owners should not be tipped because they set their own prices and retain profits. In modern salon culture, however, this rule has largely disappeared. If the salon owner personally stands on their feet for 45 minutes to cut, wash, and style your hair, tipping the standard 15% to 20% is expected and appreciated.

### Cash vs. card: which is preferred for haircut tips?
While modern POS systems allow tipping via credit card, barbers and stylists overwhelmingly prefer **cash tips**. Credit card processing companies deduct 2.5% to 3.5% in merchant transaction fees from card tips, and card gratuities are often delayed until biweekly payroll.
