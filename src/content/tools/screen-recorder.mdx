## How to Record Your Screen in 3 Steps

Capture high-definition video of your screen and audio directly in your browser:

1. **Click Start Recording:** Press the red **Start Recording Screen** button above to launch your browser's native screen sharing selector.
2. **Choose Source & Audio:** Select whether to capture your entire desktop monitor, a specific application window, or a single browser tab. Check the audio box to record system sounds or your microphone.
3. **Save Video Instantly:** Click **Stop Recording & Save** when finished. Preview the recording and click **Download Recording (WebM)** to save the video file directly to your disk.

### Practical Screen Recording Scenarios

- **Software Bug Reporting:** Record steps to reproduce a software glitch to share with development and engineering teams.
- **Client & Student Tutorials:** Create clear video walkthroughs of digital products, spreadsheets, or online tools.
- **Presentation Recording:** Record slide demonstrations with voiceovers without installing heavy video editing suites.
