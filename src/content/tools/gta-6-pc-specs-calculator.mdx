## Why 200GB+ NVMe SSD storage is critical

Grand Theft Auto VI features dynamic crowd density, volumetric coastal weather, and seamless indoor-outdoor transitions without traditional loading screens. Mechanical hard disk drives (HDDs) operating at 150 MB/s or SATA solid-state drives capped at 500 MB/s cannot stream high-resolution mipmaps fast enough during high-speed vehicle chases.

DirectStorage-compatible PCIe Gen 4 or Gen 5 NVMe SSDs with sequential transfer rates exceeding 5,000 MB/s allow the graphics pipeline to stream uncompressed textures directly from storage into GPU memory. When planning your drive layout, reserve a dedicated 270 GB to 300 GB partition to provide sufficient buffer for shader compilation caches, virtual memory swap files, and cumulative patch installations. ## The VRAM bottleneck at 1440p and 4K UHD

Video random-access memory (VRAM) is the primary constraint for modern open-world graphics engines. Ray-traced reflections across wet asphalt, high-resolution shadow cascades, and 4K texture packages fill GPU frame buffers rapidly:

- **1080p Full HD:** 8 GB VRAM delivers stable frame pacing on medium-to-high graphical presets.
- **1440p Quad HD & 21:9 Ultrawide:** 12 GB VRAM is recommended to prevent frame drops caused by texture swapping into system RAM. Test panoramic display ratios with our [Aspect Ratio Calculator](/aspect-ratio-calculator/).
- **4K Ultra Ray Tracing:** 16 GB to 24 GB VRAM is necessary to run native 3840x2160 rendering without severe memory overcommitment.

If your graphics card is limited to 8 GB or 10 GB of VRAM, utilize temporal upscalers such as DLSS or FSR to render frames at a lower internal resolution while reconstructing crisp edges.
