## Can my PC run GTA 6?

As of November 26, 2026, Rockstar lists GTA VI for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Its platform listing gives no Windows system requirements. You can compare your PC with the estimated targets here, but a score cannot confirm that the game will launch or reach 60 FPS. [Rockstar's GTA VI platform listing](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI)

## Where do I find my PC specs on Windows?

Press **Ctrl + Shift + Esc** to open Task Manager, then select **Performance**. Under **CPU**, use the number labeled **Cores**. Under **GPU**, use the capacity shown for **Dedicated GPU memory**. For example, a reading of 2.1/8.0 GB means the card has 8 GB. Under **Memory**, read the installed RAM separately. For free disk space, open **This PC** in File Explorer and check the drive where you would install the game.

## What does the GTA 6 FPS calculator estimate?

Choose a resolution and a target of 30, 60 or 120 FPS. The calculator compares your CPU core count, video memory, system memory and storage with its built-in thresholds. FPS is an input here. The score tells you how closely your hardware fits the selected scenario. It cannot tell you how many frames a particular GPU produces in the game.

## Why two graphics cards with the same VRAM can score alike

The calculator asks for video memory rather than a GPU model. Two cards with 8 GB of memory can therefore receive the same memory assessment even if one renders a scene much faster. CPU generation, memory bandwidth, driver version and graphics settings can also affect measured frame times. Check the resolution used by a benchmark when comparing it with your own display.

## How to compare two setups

Keep your resolution and target FPS fixed and change one input at a time. For example, compare 8 GB and 16 GB of VRAM with the same CPU, RAM and storage. A change in score shows which threshold you crossed. It does not measure a performance gain from buying that card.

## What does the storage estimate include?

Rockstar says the console installation requires up to **212 GB** depending on the device. Its figure applies to consoles. The separate PC planning estimate below includes extra room for updates. [Rockstar installation requirements](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/M6h7B5fJZMwG1dnzQkt4Z/grand-theft-auto-vi-installation-requirements)

The planning model sets aside 215 GB for game data, 15 GB for shaders, 16 GB for virtual memory and 25 GB for updates. Those assumptions add up to 271 GB. With 250 GB free, the calculator shows a 21 GB shortfall against that planning figure. For platform availability and publisher specifications, consult the [official Grand Theft Auto VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI).
