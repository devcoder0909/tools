## Instant Online Character and Word Counter

Whether you are crafting a social media post, writing an academic paper, or optimizing SEO meta tags, staying within character constraints is critical. Our character counter updates in real time:

- **Characters (with spaces):** Measures every character, symbol, punctuation mark, and whitespace.
- **Characters (no spaces):** Counts purely alphanumeric characters and punctuation without spaces.
- **Total Word Count:** Real-time word calculation based on whitespace and delimiter detection.
- **Estimated Reading Time:** Calculated at the standard human reading rate of 200 words per minute.
