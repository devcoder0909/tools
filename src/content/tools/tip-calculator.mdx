## How to Calculate Restaurant Tips and Split Bills

Tipping etiquette and calculation formulas are straightforward:

1. **Calculate Tip Amount:**
   $$\text{Tip} = \text{Bill Amount} \times \left(\frac{\text{Tip \%}}{100}\right)$$
2. **Calculate Total Bill:**
   $$\text{Total Bill} = \text{Bill Amount} + \text{Tip}$$
3. **Calculate Per Person Share:**
   $$\text{Per Person Total} = \frac{\text{Total Bill}}{\text{Number of People}}$$

*Worked Example:* A dinner bill of \$75.00 with an 18% tip split among 3 friends:
- Total Tip: $\$75.00 \times 0.18 = \$13.50$
- Total Bill: $\$75.00 + \$13.50 = \$88.50$
- Per Person Share: $\$88.50 \div 3 = \$29.50$ (includes $\$4.50$ tip per guest)
