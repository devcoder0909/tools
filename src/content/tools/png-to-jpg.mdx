## PNG to JPG for upload forms: what to check

Convert PNG to JPG when a website accepts JPEG photos but rejects PNG files. Changing a filename from `.png` to `.jpg` is not conversion: the image must be decoded and saved using JPEG encoding. Both `.jpg` and `.jpeg` identify JPEG images.

## JPEG vs JPG: What Is the Difference?

A common question among web creators and photographers is whether JPEG and JPG are different file formats:

- **They Are the Exact Same Format:** Both `.jpeg` and `.jpg` refer to the Joint Photographic Experts Group standard (ISO/IEC 10918-1). The underlying binary encoding, Discrete Cosine Transform compression, and color profiles are identical.
- **Why Do Two Extensions Exist?** Early versions of MS-DOS and Windows (Windows 95 and earlier) enforced the 8.3 file naming convention, allowing only a 3-letter file extension (`.jpg`). Meanwhile, Unix, Linux, and Apple Macintosh operating systems had no 3-character limitation and used `.jpeg`.
- **Can You Simply Rename .jpeg to .jpg?** Yes. Renaming the extension does not corrupt the image because the file headers are identical. However, converting through ZixTool also lets you compress, optimize quality, and resize dimensions in one step.

## Frequently Encountered Questions & Troubleshooting

### Why is my converted JPG larger than the original PNG?
If your source PNG consists of simple graphics with flat colors (such as a 2-color icon or line art), PNG's Deflate indexing algorithm is already exceptionally compact. Converting simple flat vector-like art to JPEG can introduce high-frequency DCT noise that actually increases byte count. We recommend keeping simple logos in PNG format and reserving JPG conversion for screenshots, photos, and complex illustrations.

### Why did my transparent background turn black in some viewers?
Certain older image viewers render transparent pixels as black when JPEG metadata lacks explicit color space tags. ZixTool automatically composites an opaque pure white background before JPEG encoding, ensuring consistent white background display across every viewer, mobile device, and web browser.

### How do I preserve full image resolution without downsampling?
ZixTool never scales down your image dimensions unless you deliberately select resize settings. Your converted JPG retains the exact pixel width and height of the original PNG file.
