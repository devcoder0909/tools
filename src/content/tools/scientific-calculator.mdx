## How to Use the Scientific Calculator in 3 Steps

Solve complex algebraic, trigonometric, and engineering equations in seconds:

1. **Enter Mathematical Expression:** Use the on-screen keypad or your computer's keyboard to enter numbers, operators (`+`, `-`, `×`, `÷`), parentheses, and constants like $\pi$ or $e$.
2. **Apply Scientific Functions:** Click specialized trigonometric keys (`sin`, `cos`, `tan`), logarithmic functions (`ln`, `log`), roots (`sqrt`), or exponents (`^`).
3. **Calculate Result:** Press the `=` button or press `Enter` on your keyboard to evaluate the expression instantly with full floating-point precision.

### Key Mathematical Capabilities

- **Trigonometry (Radians & Degrees):** Toggle between Radian mode (for calculus and engineering physics) and Degree mode (for geometry and navigation).
- **Logarithms & Powers:** Calculate natural logarithms ($\ln x$, base $e$), common logarithms ($\log_{10} x$), and arbitrary powers ($x^y$).
- **Order of Operations:** Automatically enforces standard operator precedence rules (PEMDAS / BODMAS) with unlimited nested parentheses.
