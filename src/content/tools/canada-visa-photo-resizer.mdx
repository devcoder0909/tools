## Official IRCC Canadian visa & passport photo specifications

When uploading digital photographs to the IRCC online application portal, files must strictly comply with official Canadian immigration guidelines:

- **Dimensions:** $35\text{ mm wide} \times 45\text{ mm high} (1\frac{3}{8}\text{ in} \times 1\frac{3}{4}\text{ in})$.
- **Digital Resolution:** Minimum $420 \times 540\text{ pixels}$ at 300 DPI (recommended: $600 \times 771\text{ pixels}$).
- **File Size Range:** Minimum **60 KB**, maximum **240 KB** (target size: ~180KB–220KB).
- **File Format:** JPEG or JPEG2000 (.jpg or .jpeg).
- **Head Size Proportions:** The distance from the chin to the crown of the head must measure between $31\text{ mm}$ and $36\text{ mm}$ (70% to 80% of total vertical height).
- **Background Tone:** Plain white or light-coloured background with no textures, patterns, or shadows.
- **Facial Expression:** Neutral facial expression with mouth closed and eyes looking directly into the camera lens.

## IRCC photo compliance checklist

Before uploading your photo to the Canadian immigration system, ensure that:

- **Lighting is Even:** There are no reflections or glare on the face, forehead, or cheekbones.
- **No Head Coverings:** Religious head coverings are permitted provided the full facial oval from the chin to the top of the forehead remains completely visible.
- **Clear Face Visibility:** Tinted sunglasses and heavy spectacle frames are prohibited; eyes must be clearly visible without glare reflections on the lenses.
- **Recent Portrait:** The photo must have been taken within the last 6 months to reflect your current physical appearance.
