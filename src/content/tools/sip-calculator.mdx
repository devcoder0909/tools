### The Power of Compounding via SIP

Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) leverage dollar-cost averaging and exponential compound interest. The standard future value formula is:

$$M = P \times \frac{(1 + i)^n - 1}{i} \times (1 + i)$$

Where:
- **M** = Total maturity value at the end of the term
- **P** = Monthly installment amount
- **i** = Periodic monthly interest rate ($\frac{R}{12 \times 100}$)
- **n** = Total number of monthly installments

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the advantage of a SIP over a lump-sum investment?
A SIP enables rupee/dollar-cost averaging, purchasing more units when market prices drop and fewer units when prices rise, reducing volatility risk.

### Does this calculator guarantee actual returns?
No. Mutual fund and equity returns fluctuate based on broader market performance. Projections are mathematical models based on the expected annual return rate.
