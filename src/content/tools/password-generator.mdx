### Password Entropy & Strength Ratings

- **Moderate Strength (40-60 bits):** Suitable for low-risk discussion forums and disposable online accounts.
- **Strong (60-80 bits):** Recommended baseline for personal email, streaming subscriptions, and e-commerce accounts.
- **Very Strong (80-128+ bits):** Mandatory for primary email accounts, password manager master keys, cryptocurrency wallets, and banking logins.

## Why Local Browser Crypto Matters

When you use conventional online password generators, your new credentials may be generated on a remote web server and transmitted across the internet, exposing you to server logging, proxy interception, and telemetry collection:

- **Browser Native Web Crypto:** Best Converter uses `window.crypto.getRandomValues()`, an operating system-level cryptographically secure pseudorandom number generator (CSPRNG).
- **Zero Network Transmission:** The password exists solely inside your device RAM. It is never transmitted to our servers or stored in any database.
- **Instant Generation:** Generates high-entropy passwords with zero latency, even when your computer is completely offline in Airplane Mode.
