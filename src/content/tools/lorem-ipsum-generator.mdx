## Why Developers and Designers Use Placeholder Text

Placeholder text has been an industry standard since the 1500s when an unknown printer scrambled a galley of type to make a type specimen book. Essential benefits include:

- **Prevents Content Bias:** Evaluators often get distracted by reading real draft text rather than judging visual hierarchy, font readability, and spacing.
- **Realistic Letter Distribution:** The Latin word structure resembles modern European and English sentence lengths, producing natural-looking typographic blocks.
- **Flexible Mockups:** Quickly fills empty database fields, card layouts, and mobile screens before final copywriting is complete.
