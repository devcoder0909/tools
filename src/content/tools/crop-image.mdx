## Why Use an Online In-Browser Image Cropper?

Cropping images is one of the most common graphic design and social media publishing tasks:

- **Social Media Sizing:** Profile pictures require 1:1 squares, YouTube thumbnails require 16:9 landscape aspect ratios, and Instagram Reels or TikTok stories require 9:16 vertical banners.
- **Removing Unwanted Elements:** Eliminate photobombers, awkward background objects, or distracting empty margins around your primary subject without complicated desktop software.
- **E-Commerce Product Photography:** Standardize product catalog images to identical square proportions so that online store listings appear professional and uniform.
- **Document & Receipt Scanning:** Trim messy desk backgrounds, shadows, and unnecessary table margins from smartphone photo scans of receipts, ID cards, and official forms.
