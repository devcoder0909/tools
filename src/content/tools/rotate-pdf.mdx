## Common Reasons to Rotate PDF Documents

Scanned and digital PDF files frequently end up in inverted or incorrect orientations due to hardware limitations:

- **Misaligned Sheet Scanners:** High-speed automatic document feeders (ADF) often ingest alternate pages upside down or in landscape orientation when scanning mixed legal and letter sizes.
- **Mobile Camera Scans:** Document scanning apps on mobile phones frequently capture receipts, contracts, and invoices sideways depending on gyroscope sensor orientation.
- **Architectural & Blueprint Drawings:** CAD schematics and financial balance sheets designed in landscape mode need rotation when merged into portrait document binders.
- **Digital Form Submissions:** Government and university portals reject upside-down or sideways PDF attachments during electronic document verification.
