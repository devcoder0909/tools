## Fixing Upside-Down Scans and Mobile Captures

Incorrectly oriented PDF files are typically caused by hardware scanning quirks:

1. **Automatic Document Feeders (ADF):** Office multi-function copiers frequently scan double-sided contracts or legal-sized sheets in alternating upside-down orientations.
2. **Smartphone Camera Scanners:** Mobile scanning apps on iPhone and Android often misinterpret device gyroscope orientation when photographing receipts on desks, saving pages rotated 90° sideways.
3. **Engineering Blueprints & Spreadsheets:** CAD diagrams and financial models created in landscape mode need to be rotated before being bound into portrait presentation books.
4. **Government & University Verification Portals:** Visa application centers, tax authorities, and university admission boards automatically reject inverted documents during automated OCR verification.

## Frequently Encountered Issues & Troubleshooting

### Can I rotate a single page instead of all pages?
If you have a document where only one page is sideways, you can first use our [Split PDF Tool](/split-pdf/) to extract the misoriented page, rotate it, and then combine the document back together with our [Merge PDF Tool](/merge-pdf/).

### Why did my PDF open sideways in a mobile viewer after rotating?
Certain legacy mobile PDF viewers cache previously rendered page thumbnails. Clearing your viewer cache or opening the file in a new viewer will immediately display the permanently updated orientation.

### Can I rotate password-protected PDF files?
Encrypted PDFs restrict internal dictionary modification. You must enter the password to decrypt the file before applying permanent rotation.
