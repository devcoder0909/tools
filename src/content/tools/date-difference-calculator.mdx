## Calculating Days Between Dates

Computing the exact duration between two calendar dates involves standard Gregorian calendar rules:

- **Total Calendar Days:** Standard elapsed 24-hour periods between the midnight boundary of the start date and the midnight boundary of the end date.
- **Inclusive vs Exclusive Counting:** By default, date calculators exclude the start day and include the end day. Checking the "Include end day" toggle adds 1 full day for contracts, rental periods, and court deadlines.
- **Workdays vs Weekends:** Our engine iterates through each elapsed calendar date to tally Monday through Friday business workdays versus Saturday and Sunday weekend days.
