## How many days are between two dates?

Enter the start and end dates. The calculator starts with **Include end day** checked, so both dates count. Friday, December 4 through Monday, December 7, 2026 covers four dates: Friday and Monday are weekdays, and Saturday and Sunday are weekend days.

Clear **Include end day** to measure the elapsed interval. That same Friday-to-Monday trip spans three nights. The calculator counts Friday, Saturday and Sunday in the breakdown, giving one weekday and two weekend days. Entering the dates in reverse gives the same duration.

## How many working days are in a US month?

Use the [US working days by month for 2026 and 2027](#us-working-days) for a Monday-through-Friday federal work schedule. The monthly reference starts with all weekdays and subtracts the holidays observed during that calendar month. It counts both the first and the last day when they fall on a working day.

For example, November 2026 contains 21 weekdays. Veterans Day falls on Wednesday, November 11, and Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26. Subtracting those two observed holidays leaves **19 federal working days**. A business that also closes the Friday after Thanksgiving would have 18 working days on that schedule.

For a date range entirely within 2026 and 2027, the calculator also displays federal working days after observed holidays. Outside those years, use its weekday count and subtract the closures that apply to your workplace. For November 23 through November 27, 2026, the inclusive count is five weekdays, or four working days on the federal schedule.

## What happens when both dates are the same?

With the end day included, the result is one date. A Wednesday counts as one weekday and a Saturday counts as one weekend day. With the end day excluded, the elapsed interval is zero. For a birthday calculation in years, months and days, use the [age calculator](/age-calculator/).
