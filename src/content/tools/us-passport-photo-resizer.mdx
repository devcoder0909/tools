## Official U.S. Department of State photo requirements

To prevent automated rejection by the Department of State's online image upload portal, your photograph must adhere to these biometric criteria:

- **Dimensions:** Exactly $2 \times 2\text{ inches } (51 \times 51\text{ mm})$, square $1:1$ aspect ratio.
- **Pixel Resolution:** Minimum $600 \times 600\text{ pixels}$, maximum $1200 \times 1200\text{ pixels}$ at 300 DPI.
- **File Size Ceiling:** Must be equal to or less than **240 KB** (strict limit).
- **File Format:** Standard JPEG (.jpg or .jpeg) in 24-bit sRGB color space.
- **Head Proportions:** The distance between the bottom of the chin and the top of the head must measure between 1 inch and $1\frac{3}{8}\text{ inches}$ (50% to 69% of image height).
- **Eye Position:** Eye height must be located between $1\frac{1}{8}\text{ inches}$ and $1\frac{3}{8}\text{ inches}$ (56% to 69%) from the bottom of the frame.
- **Background & Lighting:** Plain white or off-white background with uniform lighting; no shadows, glare, or filters.

## Common reasons US passport photos get rejected

Over 250,000 passport applications are delayed each year due to non-compliant photos. Common errors include:

- **Wearing Eyeglasses:** Eyeglasses of any kind (including prescription glasses) are prohibited unless accompanied by a signed doctor's medical exemption.
- **Shadows Behind the Head:** Harsh flash photography creates dark shadows on the background wall. Stand 3 to 4 feet away from the wall to eliminate shadows.
- **Incorrect File Size:** Submitting an uncompressed 5MB smartphone photo causes instant upload failure on the State Department portal. Our tool clamps the file safely under the 240KB ceiling.
- **Selfie Distortion:** Holding a smartphone camera too close causes wide-angle facial distortion. Have someone take the picture from 4 to 6 feet away using standard zoom.
