## How to Permanently Make iPhone Shoot in JPG Format

If you prefer your iPhone to take photos in standard JPEG format permanently so you don't need to convert them later:

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Scroll down and tap **Camera**.
3. Tap **Formats** at the top of the menu.
4. Select **Most Compatible** instead of *High Efficiency*.
5. Future photos taken by your camera will be saved natively as `.jpg` files (note: 4K 60fps video and cinematic mode require High Efficiency).

## HEIC vs JPG Format Comparison

The table below contrasts key technical specifications between Apple's HEIC container and the universal JPEG standard:

| Feature / Specification | HEIC (High Efficiency Container) | JPG / JPEG (Universal Standard) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Compression Algorithm** | Modern HEVC (H.265) intra-frame video coding | Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) lossy encoding |
| **Typical File Size** | 40% to 50% smaller than JPEG at equal visual quality | Baseline standard; universal compatibility |
| **Color Bit Depth** | Up to 16-bit color depth (Display P3 wide gamut) | Standard 8-bit dynamic range (256 shades/channel) |
| **Alpha Transparency** | Supported (8-bit alpha channel) | Not supported (transparent areas render white) |
| **Windows PC Support** | Requires paid Microsoft Store extension | Natively supported by 100% of PC software |
| **Web Browser Support** | Native in Apple Safari; unsupported in Chrome/Edge/Firefox | Universally supported across all web browsers |
| **Best Used For** | iPhone camera storage conservation | Sharing, web publishing, printing, and email |
