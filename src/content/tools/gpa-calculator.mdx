## How to Calculate Your College or High School GPA

Keeping track of your semester and cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) is essential for graduation requirements, scholarships, and graduate school applications:

1. **Add Your Courses:** Enter the course names or course codes for your semester.
2. **Select Letter Grades:** Choose the letter grade earned in each class from the dropdown menu (e.g., A, A-, B+, B).
3. **Set Credit Hours:** Input the credit weight for each class (typically 3 or 4 credits for lecture courses, 1 credit for labs).
4. **View Instant GPA:** Your cumulative Grade Point Average, total credit hours, and academic honors status update immediately.

## Standard 4.0 GPA Grading Scale Formula

Colleges and universities calculate GPA by dividing total quality points by total credit hours attempted:

```
GPA = Total Quality Points / Total Credit Hours Attempted
```

Quality points for each course are calculated by multiplying the numerical grade point value by the course credit hours:

```
Course Quality Points = Grade Point Value * Course Credits
```
