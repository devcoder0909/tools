## When to Compress an Image to 100KB

A 100KB file size ceiling (102,400 bytes) is widely enforced across corporate, academic, and web platforms:

- **Employment Portals & ATS Platforms:** Enterprise Applicant Tracking Systems (such as Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and LinkedIn) require candidate profile headshots and document scans under 100KB to ensure fast resume loading for recruiters.
- **University Admissions & Student ID Systems:** Higher education registration portals require applicant photographs and ID card uploads under 100KB.
- **Email Attachments & Slide Decks:** Compressing multi-megabyte graphics to under 100KB keeps corporate emails from bouncing and prevents presentation slides from becoming sluggish.
- **Website Avatars & Profile Pictures:** Community forums and web applications cap profile avatars at 100KB to optimize initial page loading and Core Web Vitals.
