## Tips for Passing Automated Portal Photo Verification

Most modern government and university portals use automated computer vision scripts to validate uploaded images before accepting an application:

1. **Clean Plain Light Background:** Stand against a plain white or light off-white wall without shadows or wall textures.
2. **Centered Face Proportion:** Ensure your face occupies between 70% and 80% of the vertical frame height. Looking directly into the camera with both ears visible is recommended.
3. **Preserve Signature Contrast:** When scanning your signature, sign with a dark blue or black pen on unlined white paper. Crop tightly around the ink to dedicate maximum byte budget to the signature strokes.
4. **Strict File Size Verification:** If a portal mandates "under 50KB", an image of 50.1KB (51,300 bytes) will be rejected. Our binary search engine guarantees the output file stays strictly within the mandated limit.
