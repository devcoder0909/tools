## Technical Mathematics: Converting Physical Millimeters to Screen Pixels

When taking photographs with a smartphone or DSLR, images are measured in digital screen pixels. Official passport specifications, however, define dimensions in metric millimeters ($mm$) or inches ($in$). The mathematical transformation linking physical print dimensions to digital raster pixels is dictated by the scanning or rendering Dots Per Inch (DPI):

$$\text{Width in Pixels} = \left(\frac{\text{Width in Millimeters}}{25.4}\right) \times \text{DPI}$$

$$\text{Height in Pixels} = \left(\frac{\text{Height in Millimeters}}{25.4}\right) \times \text{DPI}$$

### Practical DPI Resolution Benchmarks for $35\text{mm} \times 45\text{mm}$ Photos:
- **High-Resolution Print Quality (300 DPI):**
  $$\text{Width} = \left(\frac{35}{25.4}\right) \times 300 = 413.38 \approx \mathbf{413\text{ px}}$$
  $$\text{Height} = \left(\frac{45}{25.4}\right) \times 300 = 531.49 \approx \mathbf{531\text{ px}}$$
- **Standard Web Portal Resolution (200 DPI):**
  $$\text{Width} = \left(\frac{35}{25.4}\right) \times 200 = 275.59 \approx \mathbf{276\text{ px}}$$
  $$\text{Height} = \left(\frac{45}{25.4}\right) \times 200 = 354.33 \approx \mathbf{354\text{ px}}$$

## Biometric Facial Geometry: Crown-to-Chin Ratio Guidelines

Modern immigration e-Gates and automated recruitment filters employ Machine Readable Travel Document (MRTD) facial recognition algorithms aligned with ICAO Document 9303 standards. To ensure compliance, align your portrait with the following structural rules:

```
+------------------------------------+  0% (Top of Frame)
|          Background Area           |
|         .----------------.         |
|        /   Hair / Crown   \        | 10% - 15% Head Margin
|       |    (Eye Level)     |       |
|       |   [ O ]   [ O ]    |       | 50% - 60% Eye Axis
|       |         ^          |       |
|        \      =====       /        |
|         '----------------'         | 70% - 80% Chin Line
|           |            |           |
|         /                \         |
|       /    Upper Torso     \       | 100% (Bottom of Frame)
+------------------------------------+
```

1. **The Eye Axis:** The horizontal plane running through the center of both pupils must sit between $50\%$ and $60\%$ of the total vertical image height from the bottom border.
2. **Crown-to-Chin Proportion:** The distance from the highest point of the hair (or top of the skull if bald) to the bottom edge of the chin must measure between $70\%$ and $80\%$ of total frame height (typically 32mm to 36mm on a 45mm print).
3. **Head Centering:** The vertical axis of the nose bridge must align within $2\%$ of the image's mathematical center, with both left and right ears equally visible.
4. **Neutral Expression:** Avoid teeth-baring smiles, tilted heads, squinted eyes, or furrowed brows. Keep lips naturally closed and gaze directed straight into the lens.

More than $30\%$ of exam application rejections stem from non-compliant digital uploads. Follow this verified technical checklist before submitting your file:

- **1. Eliminate Eyeglass Glare and Tinted Lenses:** If wearing prescription spectacles, ensure the frames do not obscure your eyes and the glass produces zero flash reflection. Tinted sunglasses or colored cosmetic contact lenses are universally prohibited.
- **2. Avoid Uneven Lighting and Heavy Drop Shadows:** Flash photography frequently casts harsh dark shadows behind the subject's ears or across the neck. Position your light source symmetrically or take your photograph facing an indirect window on an overcast day.

Enforcing a $20\text{ KB}$ to $50\text{ KB}$ limit guarantees rapid page loads and database efficiency while retaining sufficient resolution for visual identity verification.
