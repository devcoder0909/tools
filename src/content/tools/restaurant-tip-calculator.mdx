## How to calculate restaurant tips quickly: formulas and percentages

Calculating gratuity on a restaurant bill follows standard hospitality etiquette benchmarks in the United States and North America:

- **15% Gratuity (Standard / Baseline Service):** Multiply the pre-tax food and beverage subtotal by $0.15$. A $50.00 subtotal yields a $7.50 tip, resulting in a $57.50 total before tax.
- **18% Gratuity (Good / Attentive Service):** Multiply the subtotal by $0.18$. For a $75.00 dining bill, an 18% tip is $13.50.
- **20% Gratuity (Great / Exceptional Service):** Multiply the subtotal by $0.20$. The quickest mental calculation: move the decimal point one place to the left and double the number. On an $80.00 check, 10% is $8.00, so 20% is exactly $16.00.
- **25% Gratuity (Outstanding Hospitality or Large Groups):** Multiply the subtotal by $0.25$, or simply divide the subtotal by 4.

## Should you calculate tip before or after sales tax?

Etiquette experts, including the Emily Post Institute, recommend calculating gratuity strictly on the **pre-tax subtotal** of your meal. Sales tax is an obligatory municipal, state, or federal levy rather than payment for dining hospitality. Adding a tip percentage to the post-tax amount means you are tipping on government tax assessments. 

Our restaurant calculator gives you complete flexibility: you can input the food subtotal, specify your local sales tax rate, and choose whether gratuity is computed before or after tax.
