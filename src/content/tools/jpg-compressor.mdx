## How JPEG Compression Works in Your Browser

JPEG uses the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) algorithm to compress visual information:

- **Chrominance Subsampling:** The human eye is significantly more sensitive to brightness (luminance) than to subtle color nuances (chrominance). JPEG compression compresses color channels more aggressively while preserving fine luminance edges.
- **Quantization Matrices:** When you select an 80% to 90% quality setting, high-frequency spatial noise is rounded off, significantly reducing byte count without softening visible photo details.
- **Client-Side RAM Processing:** Unlike traditional online compressors that upload your files to external cloud servers, ZixTool performs all image decoding, quantization, and encoding locally in your browser memory.

## Preserving Visual Fidelity When Compressing JPGs

To ensure your compressed JPG photos look crisp and sharp:
- **Use 85% Quality for General Photos:** 85% provides the optimal balance, reducing file size by 60% to 75% while keeping optical artifacts invisible.
- **Preserve Native Resolution:** Our compressor maintains full pixel dimensions unless you explicitly choose to downsample width and height.
- **Avoid Re-compressing Multiple Times:** Re-saving an already compressed JPEG repeatedly introduces generational artifacts. Start with your highest resolution original photo for best results.
