## How much do you tip in Canada? Standard gratuity guidelines

Tipping expectations in Canadian dining establishments generally reflect the following service standards:

- **15% Gratuity (Standard / Good Service):** The customary baseline for sit-down restaurant dining across Canadian cities like Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, and Calgary.
- **18% Gratuity (Attentive / High Quality Service):** A common benchmark for prompt, courteous hospitality in busy restaurants and bars.
- **20% Gratuity (Exceptional / Fine Dining):** Given for outstanding attention to detail, personalized wine service, or large celebratory gatherings.
- **10% to 12% (Buffets & Casual Counter Dining):** Applied when table clearing and drink refills are provided at self-service buffets.

## Why you should tip on the pre-tax subtotal in Canada

A frequent complaint among Canadian diners is that handheld payment terminals (such as Moneris, Clover, and Square) calculate default percentage prompts on the **post-tax total**. 

In provinces with high sales taxes like Ontario (13% HST), Nova Scotia (15% HST), and Quebec (14.975% GST + QST), tipping 18% on the terminal's post-tax prompt can add an extra 2.5% to 3.5% in unearned gratuity. Tipping on the subtotal ensures that waitstaff are compensated fairly for their dining service while preventing you from paying a tip on government tax levies.
