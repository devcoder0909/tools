## How many words are in my draft?

Paste the text you plan to submit. Include a heading, caption or reference list only if your publisher counts it toward the limit. The totals update as you edit, so you can remove a sentence and see how much space you recover.

For an essay with a 500-word maximum, enter 500 in **Word limit**. A 487-word draft leaves 13 words for edits. Adding a 20-word sentence takes the draft to 507 words, and the counter shows you need to cut seven. You can keep typing while over the limit.

The counter splits on whitespace. Spaces, tabs and line breaks separate words. Multiple spaces between two words still give a total of two. Try ice cream and then ice-cream. The first has two words and the second has one. A standalone dash surrounded by spaces counts as a separate item. For languages that do not separate words with spaces, this method can undercount the words in a passage.

## Characters with and without spaces

For the phrase red fox, the total is seven characters including the space and six excluding it. A line break also counts toward the total. The count excluding whitespace removes it. The character total uses JavaScript string length, so some emoji contribute more than one unit. When a form enforces a strict limit, confirm the final count in the destination field.

## Estimate a reading or speaking time

The reading estimate divides the word total by 200. The speaking estimate uses 130 words per minute. Each rounds up to whole minutes. For a 400-word script, the estimates are two minutes to read and four minutes to speak. Pause lengths and delivery speed vary, so time a rehearsal for a scheduled talk.

## Why two editors can disagree

A difference of a few words often comes from hyphens, headings or punctuation. Copy the same paragraph into both editors, then compare one sentence at a time. For a character limit, check whether each editor includes trailing spaces and line breaks. When comparing revisions, use the [text diff checker](/diff-checker/) to locate the words that changed.
