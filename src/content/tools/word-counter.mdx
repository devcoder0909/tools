## Real-Time Text Analysis & Metrics

Our browser-based word counter analyzes your writing as you type, calculating key composition metrics instantly:

- **Word Count:** Total words separated by standard whitespace boundaries.
- **Character Count (With & Without Spaces):** Total typographic glyphs, essential for social media character caps.
- **Sentences & Paragraphs:** Structural units identified by terminal punctuation (. ? !) and line break delimiters.
- **Reading Time:** Estimated based on the universal reading benchmark of 200 words per minute (WPM).
- **Speaking Time:** Estimated based on the professional speech pacing standard of 130 words per minute.
