### Understanding the Standard EMI Formula

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is calculated using the globally accepted financial formula:

$$EMI = \frac{P \times r \times (1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}$$

Where:
- **P** = Principal loan amount
- **r** = Monthly interest rate (Annual interest rate divided by 12 months, expressed as a decimal: $\frac{R}{12 \times 100}$)
- **n** = Total loan duration in months (Years $\times 12$)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens when I increase my EMI payment?
Making extra principal payments or choosing a shorter tenure substantially reduces the compound interest charged by your bank over the lifetime of the loan.

### Can I calculate both fixed and floating loan EMIs?
Yes. The calculated EMI applies to any standard reducing-balance loan schedule.
