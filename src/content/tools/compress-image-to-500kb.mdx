## Frequently Asked Questions About 500KB Image Compression

In most cases, no. Because a 500KB budget is generous, our algorithm preserves full native pixel resolution (such as 4000 × 3000 pixels) and achieves the target solely by fine-tuning the discrete cosine transform (DCT) quantization tables. Downscaling is only applied if a raw image exceeds 30 megapixels.
