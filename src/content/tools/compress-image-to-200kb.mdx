## Common Errors & Troubleshooting for Portal Uploads

- **"File size exceeds 200KB" Portal Rejection:** Some legacy government portals calculate 1KB as 1,000 bytes rather than 1,024 bytes (binary kibibyte). If a strict portal rejects a 200KB file, set our custom target input to `190KB` or `195KB` to provide a safety margin.
- **Blurred Text on Scanned Documents:** If you are compressing a scanned certificate, mark sheet, or passport copy, ensure the original scan was captured at a minimum of 200 DPI to 300 DPI. Our engine preserves text clarity by avoiding excessive downscaling when fitting inside 200KB.
- **Color Shifting (P3 to sRGB):** High-end smartphones (iPhone 12 through iPhone 16 Pro) capture photos in Apple Display P3 wide color gamut. ZixTool automatically remaps P3 color space coordinates into standard sRGB, ensuring your photos look natural and vibrant across all web browsers without color tint distortion.
