## How Percentage Calculations Work

A percentage represents a proportion expressed as a fraction of 100. Our in-browser calculator supports three distinct calculation modes:

1. **Finding the Percentage of a Given Number:**
   $$\text{Result} = \left(\frac{P}{100}\right) \times V$$
   *Example:* To find 15% of 240: $(15 \div 100) \times 240 = 0.15 \times 240 = 36$.

2. **Calculating What Percentage X is of Y:**
   $$\text{Percentage} = \left(\frac{X}{Y}\right) \times 100$$
   *Example:* If a student scores 42 points out of 50: $(42 \div 50) \times 100 = 0.84 \times 100 = 84\%$.

3. **Percentage Increase or Decrease Between Two Numbers:**
   $$\text{Percentage Change} = \left(\frac{V_2 - V_1}{V_1}\right) \times 100$$
   *Example:* An item increases from \$80 to \$100: $((100 - 80) \div 80) \times 100 = (20 \div 80) \times 100 = +25\%$.
