## When did GTA 6 come out in the US?

Grand Theft Auto VI came out on Thursday, November 19, 2026. Rockstar announced worldwide availability that day for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The US console release shares that November date. [Rockstar's launch announcement](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39akk424a9oo53/grand-theft-auto-vi-now-available)

## How many days has GTA 6 been out?

The counter above uses November 19 as day zero. On November 20, one calendar day had passed. On November 26, seven calendar days had passed, marking one week since launch. This count advances with the date, so someone checking just after midnight can see a different number from someone still on the previous day.

If you started playing later and want to count the days since your own first session, choose your start and end dates in the [date difference calculator](/date-difference-calculator/).

## What time did GTA 6 unlock?

Rockstar scheduled the digital console release for midnight local time on November 19. For US players, that meant midnight arrived on the East Coast before it reached the West Coast. A preload let players download game files ahead of the unlock. [Rockstar's regional release schedule](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s)

Rockstar's console launch announcement covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
