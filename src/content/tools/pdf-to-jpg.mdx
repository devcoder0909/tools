## Why Convert PDF Documents to JPG?

While PDF format is standard for document exchange, JPEG images are often required for specific workflows:

- **Social Media & Messaging:** Platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp do not allow direct PDF previews in image carousels. Converting to JPG makes sharing visual slides instant.
- **Office Presentations & Word Documents:** Pasting a high-resolution JPG image into PowerPoint slides, Google Docs, or Word files is smoother than embedding multi-page PDF attachments.
- **Online Job Portals & KYC Verification:** Many government, bank, and job application forms restrict document uploads strictly to `.jpg` or `.jpeg` formats.

## Comparison: PDF Document vs JPG Image Format

| Feature | PDF Format | JPG / JPEG Image Format |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **File Structure** | Compound binary container (text, vector, fonts) | Raster compressed pixel bitmap |
| **Universal Image Viewer Support** | Requires PDF reader software or plugin | Natively opens on every phone, tablet, and PC |
| **Embedding in Web Pages** | Requires `<iframe>` or heavy JS viewers | Standard `<img>` tag with instant loading |
| **Editing Ease** | Requires dedicated PDF editor | Easy to crop, annotate, or filter in photo editors |
| **Social Media Compatibility** | Limited to document sharing | Native photo gallery and feed support |
