## Why Convert PDF Documents to JPG Images?

While PDF format is standard for document archival, converting pages into JPG images is essential for common digital workflows:

- **Social Media & Messaging Apps:** Platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp do not allow direct PDF previews in image carousels. Converting to JPG makes sharing visual slides, receipts, and infographics effortless.
- **Office Presentations & Word Documents:** Inserting a high-resolution JPG into PowerPoint slides, Google Docs, or Word files is smoother than embedding multi-page PDF attachments.
- **Online Portals & Identity Verification:** Many government, visa, and banking recruitment portals restrict document uploads strictly to `.jpg` or `.jpeg` formats below 200KB or 50KB.
- **Fast Web Embedding:** Loading a JPEG image using a standard HTML `<img>` tag is significantly faster for website visitors than embedding heavy PDF viewer plugins.

| **Editing & Cropping** | Requires specialized PDF editor software | Easily cropped, filtered, or resized in any photo tool |
