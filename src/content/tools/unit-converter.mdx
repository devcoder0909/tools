## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the temperature conversion formula work?
To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply by $9/5$ (or 1.8) and add 32: $F = (C \times 1.8) + 32$.

### Does the digital storage converter use binary or decimal units?
This calculator uses binary measurement ($1\text{ KB} = 1,024\text{ Bytes}$ and $1\text{ MB} = 1,024\text{ KB}$) standard in computer operating systems.
