## How Page Size and Layout Settings Affect Your Output

Choosing the right page settings ensures your document looks neat, readable, and ready for physical printing or digital sharing:

### A4 vs. US Letter vs. Fit to Image
- **A4 ($210 \times 297\text{ mm}$ / $595 \times 842\text{ pt}$):** The international standard used across Europe, Asia, Latin America, and Africa. Best for academic papers, official forms, and international correspondence.
- **US Letter ($8.5 \times 11\text{ inches}$ / $612 \times 792\text{ pt}$):** The dominant paper standard in the United States, Canada, and parts of Mexico. Ideal for North American business filings.
- **Fit to Image:** The PDF page dimensions automatically match the pixel dimensions and aspect ratio of each source image. This mode is ideal for digital art portfolios, panoramic captures, and visual mockups where you do not want white margins around the edges. To calculate custom dimensional ratios, verify proportions with our [Aspect Ratio Calculator](/aspect-ratio-calculator/).

### Setting Margins to Protect Printable Content
Standard desktop printers have mechanical unprintable margins along paper edges (typically $3\text{ mm}$ to $5\text{ mm}$). When preparing receipts or legal documents for physical printing, enabling margins prevents essential details—such as timestamps, page numbers, and signatures—from being clipped by printer feed rollers.
