## The Formula Behind Compound Interest Calculation

Compound interest represents interest computed on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from prior periods. The standard mathematical formula is:

$$A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{n \times t}$$

Where the mathematical variables correspond to:
- **$A$**: The accrued future value balance at maturity.
- **$P$**: The initial principal deposit amount.
- **$r$**: The annual nominal interest rate expressed as a decimal ($8\% = 0.08$).
- **$n$**: The number of compounding intervals per calendar year (e.g., $12$ for monthly).
- **$t$**: The overall tenure of the investment measured in years.
