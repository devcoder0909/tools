## Understanding the Standard Loan Amortization Formula

The monthly payment for fixed-rate installment loans is computed using the standard compound amortization formula:

```
M = P * [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]
```

- **M:** Monthly Payment (EMI)
- **P:** Loan Principal Amount
- **r:** Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate divided by 12 and 100)
- **n:** Total Number of Monthly Payments (Years multiplied by 12)
