## Meeting Official Portal & Government Upload Requirements

Official submission forms, recruitment boards, and civil service portals strictly enforce byte ceilings. If your file exceeds the maximum limit by even 1 byte, the portal rejects your submission:

- **Passport & Visa Photos (20KB to 50KB):** International visa systems, passport application forms, and national ID databases commonly restrict uploads to between 20KB and 50KB with fixed pixel proportions (such as 35mm x 45mm or 600x600 px).
- **Candidate Signatures & Thumb Impressions (10KB to 20KB):** Public service exams, banking recruitment portals, and university entrance forms enforce a strict ceiling between 10KB and 20KB to ensure quick processing on low-bandwidth databases.
- **Educational Certificates & Identity Scans (100KB to 200KB):** Academic transcripts, student IDs, and verification documents are frequently capped at 100KB or 200KB.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does image compression reduce photo quality?
Lossy compression reduces file size by discarding high-frequency color variations that the human eye cannot perceive. At our recommended 80% to 90% settings, photos retain sharp facial features and crisp contours with no visible optical degradation.

### Can I decompress a compressed image to get the original back?
Lossy compression permanently discards high-frequency pixel information during encoding. While you can re-encode an image to a larger format, discarded detail cannot be mathematically restored. Always retain your original uncompressed master files.

### How do I compress an image to passport size?
Upload your portrait, choose the **50KB** target size preset (or use our [Passport Photo Resizer](/passport-photo-resizer/)), and ensure your image proportions match standard passport guidelines (typically 35mm x 45mm).
