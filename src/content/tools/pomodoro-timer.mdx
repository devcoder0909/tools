## How to Boost Your Productivity with the Pomodoro Timer

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management methodology created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s that uses structured work sprints:

1. **Pick a Single Task:** Choose one specific assignment, programming feature, or study chapter you want to complete.
2. **Start the 25-Minute Sprint:** Click **Start Focus** and commit 100% of your attention to the task without checking emails or social media.
3. **Take a 5-Minute Break:** When the notification chimes, step away from your desk, stretch, or hydrate for 5 minutes.
4. **Repeat the Cycle:** After completing four 25-minute intervals, enjoy an extended 15-minute long break to recharge mental stamina.

---

## Core Principles of the Pomodoro Technique

Working in fixed intervals combats mental fatigue, procrastination, and multitasking inefficiencies:

- **Timeboxing:** Limiting tasks to 25 minutes creates productive urgency, preventing Parkinson’s Law (work expanding to fill the time available).
- **Reduced Burnout:** Frequent short rest intervals preserve neurological energy across multi-hour study sessions and coding marathons.
- **Progress Tracking:** Counting completed pomodoro cycles gives a concrete metric of your daily focused output.
