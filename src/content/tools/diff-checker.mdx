## How to Compare Two Texts and Find Differences Online

Comparing two versions of a document, source code file, or contract clause is straightforward:

1. **Paste Original Text:** Enter or paste the baseline text, original code, or previous draft into the left input container.
2. **Paste Modified Text:** Enter or paste the updated draft, revised agreement, or edited snippet into the right input container.
3. **Inspect Highlighted Diff:** Review the unified or side-by-side comparison view. Removed strings appear in red, while newly inserted words and lines appear in clear green.

## When to Use an Online Diff Checker Tool

Spotting minute typographical modifications, altered numbers, or omitted clauses across thousands of lines of text manually is error-prone:

- **Legal Agreements & Contract Revisions:** Verify that the counterparty did not introduce stealth modifications, altered liability clauses, or modified indemnity figures before signing.
- **Code Reviews & Script Debugging:** Inspect configuration file differences (`.env`, `.json`, `.yaml`), SQL migration queries, or code patches without firing up heavy IDE diff panels.
- **Academic & Editorial Proofreading:** Track revisions between essay drafts, translated manuscripts, and collaborative articles to review editing suggestions.
- **Plagiarism & Duplication Audits:** Contrast student submissions or blog articles against reference texts to isolate verbatim copied passages.
