Why Client-Side PDF Conversion Protects Corporate & Legal Documents

Legal agreements, bank statements, tax forms, and medical records contain sensitive personally identifiable information (PII). Uploading them to random online PDF converters poses serious compliance and GDPR risks.

On ZixTool PDF to Word and ZixTool PDF to Text, all parsing is performed inside the client memory via WebAssembly. Neither our servers nor any third party ever see your documents.
