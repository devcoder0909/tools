4. HEIC to JPG: upgraded page spec
Title (60 or fewer): HEIC to JPG Converter - Free, No Upload | Best Converter
Meta (120-158): Convert HEIC to JPG free in your browser. Photos never leave your device, no sign-up, no file limits, and you can batch convert and download as ZIP.
Only claim no file limits if the engine really has none. State the measured device limits instead.
H1: HEIC to JPG Converter
Subtitle (page-specific): Open iPhone photos anywhere. Drop HEIC files, get JPGs, nothing uploaded.
Sections: the tool; 3-step how-to as a real ordered list; iPhone and Android steps; why convert; HEIC vs JPG as a real table; measured before/after on labeled sample photos; FAQ (below); related links; reviewer and last-reviewed date.
HEIC vs JPG table rows: compression, typical file size, transparency, color depth, browser support, best for.
FAQ (12). Verify each answer before publishing:
1.  Why won't my iPhone photos open on Windows? Windows needs codec extensions from the Microsoft Store. The HEIF Image Extension is free. The HEVC Video Extensions are paid (check current price). Converting to JPG avoids both.
2.  Does converting reduce quality? HEIC is already lossy. JPG compresses again, so a little detail is lost. At 85-95% quality it is hard to see.
3.  Are my photos uploaded? No. Files are decoded in your browser and you can check the Network tab.
4.  Do Live Photos keep their motion? No. You get a still JPG. The motion is stored in a separate video file.
5.  How do I stop my iPhone saving HEIC? Settings, Camera, Formats, Most Compatible. New photos are saved as JPG. Old ones stay HEIC.
6.  Are JPG and JPEG the same? Yes. Only the extension differs.
7.  Will metadata like location stay? State what your engine actually does, and offer a strip-metadata option.
8.  Can I convert HEIF files? State exactly which extensions your engine accepts.
9.  Will the JPG be smaller than my HEIC? Often larger, since HEIC is usually the more efficient format. Show a measured range from your own samples. Do not copy a competitor's claim.
10.  Is there a file limit? State the measured device limits and the batch behavior.
11.  Why does my iPhone use HEIC? To save space at similar quality. Apple adopted it with iOS 11 in 2017.
12.  How do I open HEIC on Mac or Android? Apple software opens it natively. Check current Android support before stating details.
Measurement rule: run your own engine on 5-10 fixture photos, record before and after sizes, and label them sample results. Never invent numbers.

• E. Comparison (X vs Y)
• F. Calculator
• G. Unit or measure pair (only niche or complex pairs. Google answers common unit conversions in its own widget.)
Content standards by tier (my judgment, not a ranking law):
• Tier 1 pair page (TinyPNG standard): H1; tool; answer-first intro of 40-60 words; 3-step how-to including phone steps; why convert; X vs Y table; measured before/after numbers on labeled sample files; 8-12 real FAQs with short, direct answers; 8-12 curated related links; last-reviewed date. Roughly 900-1,300 words.
• Tier 2: about 500-800 words, 5-6 FAQs.
• Tier 3: at least 350 unique words, 3-4 FAQs.
• Calculator and unit pages: formula, worked example, static lookup table of 8-12 rows, 4-6 FAQs, reverse link.
• FAQ questions must be unique per page (at most 30% shared with any other page). Before/after numbers must be measured on our own fixtures by our own engine. Never invent them.
8. On-page rules
Title formulas (60 characters or fewer, unique):
• Pair: {From} to {To} Converter - Free, No Upload | {{BRAND}}
• Target size: Compress Image to {N}KB - Free Online | {{BRAND}}
• Calculator: {Name} Calculator - Free and Accurate | {{BRAND}}
• Hub: {FMT} File: What It Is, How to Open and Convert | {{BRAND}}
Meta descriptions (120-158 characters, unique): outcome plus differentiator plus action. Example: Convert HEIC to JPG free in your browser. Photos never leave your device, no sign-up, no file limits, and you can batch convert and download as ZIP.
Other rules:
• One H1 matching the keyword. H2s phrased as the questions people search.
• Answer first, then detail. Natural use of related entities and synonyms.
• Descriptive file names and alt text. Width and height on every image.
• Internal links: 10-20 purposeful links on Tier 1 pages: reverse pair, siblings with the same source, siblings with the same target, related size pages, the format hub, one guide. Descriptive, varied anchors. Add an /all-tools/ HTML index linking every tool. Do not add 70-link mesh blocks at launch.
• Breadcrumbs on every page. Visible last-reviewed date, updated only on real changes.
9. Technical SEO and structured data
• Canonicals, sharded sitemaps with accurate lastmod, robots.txt, clean 404s, 301s, no parameter URLs, correct status codes.
• Core Web Vitals pass on mobile with ad slots reserved. JS 90KB or less, HTML 60KB or less per page. Lazy-load engines after file selection.
• i18n-ready URL structure and hreflang helper, disabled until a language launches.
Schema (valid JSON-LD only):
• WebApplication (free, UtilitiesApplication, any OS)
• BreadcrumbList on every page, Organization and WebSite on the home page
• Article on guides with author and dateModified
• FAQPage only where the FAQ is visible and genuine, with no expectation of rich results
• Never use HowTo, fake ratings or AggregateRating without real reviews
• Validate with Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator
