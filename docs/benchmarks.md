# Weekly Growth & Technical Scorecard Template

Track weekly performance metrics every Monday morning to monitor health against the milestone ladder and enforce kill/pivot rules.

## Kill / Pivot Hard Gates
- **Month 3 Gate:** If < 50% of launch pages are indexed with flat search impressions, pause new page scaffolding and fix content depth/quality.
- **Month 6 Gate:** If < 3,000 monthly organic visits with clean indexing, re-evaluate intent angles and pivot to narrower high-intent niches.
